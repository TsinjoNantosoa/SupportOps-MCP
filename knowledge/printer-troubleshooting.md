# Network Printer Troubleshooting

## Purpose
Diagnose a fictional shared printer without administrative or physical-risk actions.

## Category
Printer

## Symptoms
Printer appears offline, jobs remain queued, or several demo users cannot print.

## Preconditions
Confirm the printer asset label and obtain permission before clearing any user's test job.

## Diagnostic Steps
1. Determine whether one user or several users are affected.
2. Check the printer display for a paper, toner, or offline indication.
3. Verify the workstation can reach the documented printer test address.
4. Inspect the local queue and capture the oldest job state.
5. Compare with one authorized demo workstation.

## Recommended Actions
Correct paper state or reconnect the documented queue after confirmation. Never install unapproved drivers.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate hardware faults, multi-user outages, driver changes, or print-server symptoms.
