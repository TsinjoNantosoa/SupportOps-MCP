# Ticket Prioritization and Escalation

## Purpose
Apply consistent severity and escalation rules to fictional SupportOps tickets.

## Category
Operations

## Symptoms
Unclear priority, widening impact, repeated failure, or a required action outside N1 authority.

## Preconditions
Impact, urgency, affected users, and troubleshooting evidence are recorded.

## Diagnostic Steps
1. Separate observed facts from hypotheses and copied ticket text.
2. Determine scope, business impact, workaround availability, and security relevance.
3. Compare priority with the documented impact matrix.
4. Identify the owning team and attach reproducible evidence.
5. Record the escalation reason without including secrets.

## Recommended Actions
Raise priority only when impact and urgency justify it; route to the named specialist team with a concise evidence summary.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate critical impact, security suspicion, multi-user outage, repeated failed resolution, or any privileged change.
